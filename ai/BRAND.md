# SES ICT HUB Brand Notes

SES ICT HUB is a Nairobi electronics shop. The storefront should sound practical, clear, and human.

Visual design direction lives in `docs/design.md`. This file remains the source for voice, copy, and truthfulness rules.

## Voice

Use wording that sounds like a shop assistant speaking to a customer:

- Clear about stock, price, warranty, and condition
- Helpful without pressure
- Local to Nairobi where delivery or pickup is mentioned
- Short enough for mobile screens

Avoid copy that sounds like software marketing:

- No hype
- No fake urgency
- No "journey", "flow", "seamless", or similar process words
- No claims that are not backed by catalog data

## Customer-Facing Copy

Prefer:

- Browse Shop
- Confirm on WhatsApp
- Check availability
- Reserve now
- View details
- Same-day Nairobi delivery available
- Pickup available at our Moi Avenue shop
- We will confirm before dispatch

Avoid:

- Start journey
- Continue flow
- Proceed
- Switch to WhatsApp
- Optimize your purchase journey
- Seamless experience

## Product Truth

Only show product facts that exist in the current schema. Do not invent specs, warranty terms, stock levels, ratings, or review counts.
