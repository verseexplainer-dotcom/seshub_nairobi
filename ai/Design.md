## Brand & Style

A premium electronics ecommerce experience for Nairobi and Kenya. The interface should feel smart, professional, fast, modern, minimal, clean, and reliable.

The visual direction is a premium technology showcase: deep navy surfaces, electric blue conversion moments, soft cyan highlights, slate dividers, premium white text, and graphite depth. It should feel refined, technology-led, and commerce-ready.

## Colors

Use a calm, high-contrast tech palette.

- **Deep Navy:** The foundation for page backgrounds and hero sections.
- **Electric Blue:** Primary action color for commerce CTAs and important links.
- **Soft Cyan:** Accent color for highlights, focus states, and secondary emphasis.
- **Slate:** Dividers, muted text, and quiet structural UI.
- **Premium White:** Main copy and high-confidence surfaces.
- **Graphite:** Layered cards, navigation, and footer surfaces.

Avoid warm performance palettes, harsh warning language, and unrelated automotive cues.

## Typography

Keep `Space Grotesk` for headlines and CTAs. Keep `Hanken Grotesk` for body copy and interface text.

Use editorial luxury spacing through layout, margins, and generous line-height. Do not use tight tracking. Headlines should feel polished, premium, and modern.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is mobile-first and static-first. Use an 8px spacing rhythm, generous section gaps, and clear editorial hierarchy.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth should come from soft glass, subtle borders, and restrained shadows.

- Use translucent navy or graphite panels with `backdrop-filter` where overlays need depth.
- Keep borders subtle: slate or cyan at low opacity.
- Use minimal shadows to separate cards without making the UI feel heavy.
- Avoid heavy metallic panels, hard cast shadows, or harsh edge treatments.

## Shapes

Use a soft rounded system:

- Controls: 12px-16px radius.
- Cards and product surfaces: 16px-20px radius.
- Hero visuals and large editorial blocks: 20px-24px radius.

Avoid square, harsh corners. Laptops, smartphones, and premium electronics need softer modern surfaces.

## Components

### Buttons

Primary buttons use Electric Blue with premium white text and a subtle cyan hover/focus treatment. Secondary buttons use translucent navy surfaces with slate borders. CTAs should feel clean and confident.

### Cards & Overlays

Product cards use premium dark or light glass surfaces, subtle borders, rounded corners, and minimal shadows. Overlays should support the product visual without looking like heavy machinery panels.

### Form Inputs

Inputs use rounded borders, dark glass or premium white fills depending on context, and soft cyan focus states.

### Interactive Elements

Hero slider indicators should use thin blue/cyan progress cues. Category and brand scrollers should feel light, smooth, and easy to scan on mobile.
